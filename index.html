Kinetic aftermath

EGA Al Taweelah: 12-month smelter rebuild

Complete restoration of primary aluminium production could take up to 12 months . Al Taweelah site sustained significant damage from Iranian missile and drone attacks; site was fully evacuated and facilities entered emergency shutdown . Smelter produced 1.6 million tonnes of cast metal in 2025 . EGA holds significant metal inventories in transit and storage within UAE and internationally; these reserves expected to provide short-term support to customers . Wave 3 tail-risk locked in.

Iran formalizes Strait governance: PGSA X account live

Iran established Persian Gulf Strait Authority in May 2026, presenting it as sovereign regulatory body governing transit . Under proposed mechanism, vessels must apply to PGSA and submit Vessel Information Declaration covering ownership, insurance, crew, cargo, and routing before transit permit issued . Bitcoin payments to IRGC-linked wallets accepted, bypassing Western banking; Israel-linked vessels banned outright, US-linked and other 'hostile country' vessels facing severe restrictions . Bilateral routes emerging (India cluster transited 18 May). Coercive interdiction shifted to administrative governance.
