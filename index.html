Category status board

Six categories · 72h pulse
Pipeline Restart

East-West Pipeline Operational

Saudi Aramco restarted the East-West Pipeline on 22 September at reduced throughput. Targeting 4 million bpd (57% capacity) within days; full 7 mbpd restoration targeted 6–8 weeks (late October–early November). Three pumping stations damaged in the 11 September drone attack; repair timeline driven by infrastructure restoration. One cargo already scheduled for loading at Yanbu 22 Sept, bound for China.

Reuters (22 Sep), Bloomberg (16–22 Sep) · Tier 1
Hormuz Blockade

Transits Remain Suppressed at 38% Normal

Hormuz confirmed transits averaged 6.98 million bpd for the week ending 20 September (baseline 18.3 mbpd prewar). IRGC blockade remains active; U.S. naval blockade of Iranian ports in force since 14 July. Iran offered to reopen the strait within a week if the U.S. lifts its blockade (23 September, CNBC). No formal U.S. response or breakthrough reported; military standoff continues.

Kpler data (14–20 Sep), CNBC (23 Sep), IMF PortWatch · Tier 1
Rhine Chokepoint

Record Low Water Level at Kaub

Rhine water level at Kaub (critical commercial bottleneck) fell to 4 centimeters on 24 September—lowest on record since 1880. El Niño drought conditions; BfG expects further decline with no significant rain forecast before October. Chemicals comprise 11% of Rhine commodity traffic; shipping rates spike to €60–70/tonne (Rotterdam–Karlsruhe) from ~€45 in late June. Impacts German chemical supply chains and naphtha import chains.

Bloomberg (24 Sep), BfG data, Chemistry World, FreightWaves (22 Sep) · Tier 1
LNG Contract Extension

QatarEnergy FM Notified Through November

QatarEnergy's force-majeure notice to Edison (notified 28 August) covers cancellation of 28–29 LNG cargoes through early November, representing 3.8 bcm of natural gas (April–November window). No new extension filed 22–25 September. Buyers include Italy, Belgium, Pakistan, and Bangladesh. Total affected long-term cargoes across all buyers now ~28–29 (April–November inclusive).

Bloomberg (28 Aug), Edison GME platform, ChemAnalyst · Tier 1
Panama Canal Drought

Transit Cap Remains at 32 Vessels/Day

Panama Canal Authority maintains daily transit limit at 32 vessels (down from baseline 36–38). El Niño rainfall 34% below average May–August 2026. ACP warns capacity could drop to 29 or lower February–March 2027 if drought persists. Draft restriction from 15.2 m to 14.6 m remains in effect. Global shipping impact: importance of canal has increased due to simultaneous Hormuz blockade.

ACP regulatory notice (20 Sep), Industrial Info (21 Sep) · Tier 1
Bab el-Mandeb Status

Red Sea Corridor Stable; No New Strikes

Houthi forces control two islands north of Bab el-Mandeb (seized 14 September), 160 km from the strait. Throughput remains at ~35–40% normal. No new strike incidents reported 22–25 September. Bab el-Mandeb remains contested but not actively escalating this window. Saudi Red Sea port (Yanbu) now exposed to both pipeline damage (repaired 22 Sept) and Houthi naval threat (no recent strikes).

Windward Intelligence, Lloyd's List (14–25 Sep) · Tier 1

Today's posture · 3 actions

Operational verbs only
1. Validate East-West Pipeline Repair Trajectory — By 30 September (T+5 days)
Confirm: (a) Actual pumping rate achieved 22–25 Sept (target: 2–3 mbpd initial ramp); (b) number of pumping stations back online (target: 1+ by 25 Sept, 2+ by 30 Sept); (c) any supply-chain constraints on spare parts or technical personnel reported by Saudi Aramco or contractor. Source: Saudi Aramco operations statement, Bloomberg, S&P Global Platts OSP track. If actual throughput is <50% of target or repairs halt, escalate to Trend=Worse, Wave=L5 boundary.
2. Monitor Rhine Gauge Trajectory and Chemical Producer Maintenance Announcements — By 2 October (T+7 days)
Track: (a) Rhine water level at Kaub daily (BfG ELWIS real-time data); (b) announcements of scheduled maintenance or production rate cuts from BASF Ludwigshafen, Covestro, Evonik, or large naphtha crackers (signal: "force majeure or maintenance due to transport constraints"); (c) barge freight rate indices (Rotterdam–Karlsruhe €/tonne and Rhine surcharge premiums vs. baseline). If Kaub drops below 2 cm or >3 large European chemical producers announce maintenance by 2 Oct, escalate to Scenario C risk flag.
3. Track Iran-U.S. Diplomatic Ceasefire Signals — By 3 October (T+8 days)
Monitor: (a) U.S. State Department / Trump administration response to Iran's 23 Sept reopening offer (source: White House statements, Treasury OFAC guidance); (b) JMIC / Lloyd's List threat level change (if it drops below "Severe", signal diplomatic progress); (c) Hormuz transit count trend (if rises above 15 transits/24h average, signal blockade de-escalation). If formal ceasefire roadmap announced by 3 Oct, Scenario A probability rises to 70%; if military rhetoric escalates, Scenario C risk rises.
