Hormu­z Mari­time

Mine-strike narrative campaign intensifies

Iran's navy claimed an oil tanker caught fire on 14 November after hitting a naval mine in the eastern Strait; the IRGC Navy said two oil tankers including a supertanker exploded and caught fire after hitting mines on 13 November . Four tanker claims in 72h (unverified). Daily Hormuz transits remain in single digits to low double digits vs. pre-crisis 138/day baseline . Independent verification (UKMTO/JMIC formal notices) pending.

Threat escalation, credibility uncertain. JMIC Severe threat level sustained; Iranian claims unconfirmed by primary maritime sources.
LNG / Down­stream

Orlen cancels Qatar LNG contract — first buyer FM rescission

Poland's Orlen cancelled a long-term LNG contract with QatarEnergy, citing ongoing force majeure; it is the first cancellation by a QatarEnergy customer . The terminated agreement covered 1 million metric tons per year of LNG and was scheduled to run until 2034 . Orlen will keep a second contract with QatarEnergy for up to 1 million tonnes a year until 2034 . Wave 3 FM: physical absence of feedstock forcing buyer-side contract exit.

Tier 1 regulatory filing (11 Nov). Cascading commercial consequence of QatarEnergy Wave 3 FM. One of few buyers with explicit termination language in contract.
Saudi Oil Export

East-West Pipeline targeted; no flow disruption

Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline was targeted on 9 November, sparking a fire that emergency crews extinguished; pipeline operations continued without disruption (Tier 1 Saudi energy ministry). Saudi Arabia rerouted about 4 million barrels of crude a day through the pipeline since conflict disrupted Strait flows . Capacity 7 mbpd. Escalation signal: second attack in 72h window; resilience signal: rapid fire suppression, flow maintained.

Strategic redundancy hold. Alternative route bypass operational. Iran/proxy attack pattern sustained (~1–2/week). Yanbu exports stable.
Container Shipping

LA-Long Beach port strike deadline 20 Nov — 4 days out

The Pacific Maritime Association and ILWU Local 26 agreed to extend their labor contract through Friday, November 20, 2026 . The six terminals staffed by Local 26 watchmen handle approximately 85% of containerized cargo at Port of Long Beach and roughly 33% at Port of Los Angeles . Negotiations have been ongoing since July 2025 under the Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service; significant issues reportedly remain unresolved .

Imminent binary risk: strike or last-minute extension. Affects >50% of combined port volume. Harbor stress on top of Hormuz maritime hold.
QatarEnergy Status

LNG FM extended to end-December; buyer rescissions pile

QatarEnergy extended force majeure to at least the end of December 2026, with 38 cargoes (about 5 bcm) suspended since April . Orlen termination marks first buyer contract exit; EU storage is about 75%, the lowest for the time of year on record . QatarEnergy is in advanced talks with NextDecade over LNG from Rio Grande LNG's Train 6 (US alternative sourcing acceleration).

Restart-type FM signal: "end-Dec" means indefinite unless geopolitical breakthrough. Buyer flight to North American LNG (Cheniere, Woodside, NextDecade). Winter EU tightness certain.
Global Scope

Semiconductor DRAM supply tightens; Rhine gauge stable (no new FM)

DRAM remains a significant area of concern, with additional signs of pressure in PCB materials and selected integrated circuits; these changes are increasingly affecting not only pricing and quoted lead times, but also suppliers' ability to provide firm delivery commitments . No new pharmaceutical excipient FM declarations this cycle. Rhine low-water risk stable (no operator FM triggered 14–16 Nov).

Structural semiconductor tightness (not acute FM). Global supply-chain baseline risk stable. Non-Hormuz production-side FMs surfaced: zero this run (see REFLECTION).
