Hormuz Strait

Transits recovering but blockade operational

Strait crossings rose to 17 vessels in latest 24-hour window, up from 4 the day before , but still far below pre-war baseline of ~138 vessels daily . Iran claims "very close" to Oman deal on Strait management . IRGC strikes on commercial shipping starting July 7 suppressed southern-corridor transits .

IRGC interception ops sustained; Iran-Oman accord not yet formalized.
Rhine Transport

Record low water level disrupts chemical/fuel supply

Kaub gauge at 21 centimeters overnight, lowest since records began in 1880 . Commercial ships reduce loads by as much as 80% to avoid running aground, disrupting supply chains and making shipping more expensive . Tanker barge transport costs from Rotterdam to Karlsruhe rose to €125–€130/tonne from €60–€70 early last week .

Second-order EU-wide chemical/fuel availability pressure; low-water forecast through mid-August.
QatarEnergy LNG

FM extension to mid-October confirmed

QatarEnergy preparing to extend LNG force majeure through mid-October, with several buyers in Europe and Asia expecting formal notification in coming weeks . Edison SpA received FM extending four cargoes until early September , signaling multi-month supply curtailment.

LNG cascade to downstream Europe/Asia heating-season buyers now extends into Q4 2026.
Saudi Aramco Jazan

400 kbpd refinery repair on schedule

Jazan refinery shut July 27 after Houthi strike; repairs tentatively expected by August 15 . No restart confirmation announced; typical margin 7–10 days. Facility damage assessed as moderate (IGCC complex + tank farm).

Restart decision point: 15 August. If delayed past 22 August, escalation to Tier-1 FM risk.
Houthi Red Sea

Geographic escalation: strike 70 nm beyond declared zone

Houthis struck Saudi tanker NCC WAFA off Yanbu on August 5, reaching a vessel far outside the declared blockade zone . Strike occurred in international waters, signaling capability expansion. No cargo loss reported; crew safety unknown.

Distance escalation undermines rerouting mitigation; risk spreads to Gulf of Aden periphery.
Iran-Oman Accord

MoU talks ongoing; no formal agreement signed

Oman's Ministry stated talks are "positive and constructive," but warned Iran's continued targeting of vessels transiting the strait puts any progress at risk . Iran's Foreign Minister stated reopening subject to "other conditions," including US compliance with June MoU .

Negotiation pathway exists but contingent on geopolitical accord; no restart acceleration signal.
