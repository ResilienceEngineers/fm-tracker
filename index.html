IRGC closure effective; no diplomatic breakthrough announced.
East-West Pipeline

Saudi Arabia's bypass route — repair timeline

Two Saudi East-West Pipeline pumping stations damaged in 10 Sept drone attack . Repairs could take 3–5 weeks; officials briefed on the matter told AP . Drones reported launched from Iraq; investigation under way . Pipeline moved 2.6–4 Mbpd weekly average since late Aug . East-West had served as Saudi's main bypass with Hormuz closed . Estimated restart: late September to early October if repair timeline holds.

Dual-system crude-export stress: Hormuz + East-West offline simultaneously.
LNG / QatarEnergy

Edison FM extension to November

QatarEnergy unable to deliver five LNG cargoes to Edison scheduled Sept–Nov (notice 28 Aug per Edison / GME platform). Force majeure extended April–Sept end, now 24 cargoes total ≈3 bcm; Edison replaced 17 cargoes, 1.6 bcm . Edison holds 6.4 bcm/yr long-term contract with QatarEnergy since 2009 . Shell estimates Q damage repair to Q1 2027 . No new extension filed 16–19 Sept.

FM declarations static; Edison sourcing alternative supplies on spot market.
Red Sea / Bab el-Mandeb

Houthi activity — throughput constrained

Houthi rebels seized more islands along Red Sea shipping routes (14 Sept AP). Strait connects Red Sea to open ocean; islands place Houthis 20 miles from US base in Djibouti . Throughput estimated ~35–40% normal per prior briefing. No confirmed strikes on commercial shipping 16–19 Sept (Windward MIOC, Lloyd's List). Tanker rates remain elevated but not spiking.

Geopolitical escalation; no kinetic incident this 72h window.

Nexperia control seizure (late 2025) triggered Chinese export controls; automotive affected most . AI-driven capacity reallocation away from automotive. Not Hormuz-linked.
