Chokepoint · Phase Change

Strait of Hormuz: Toll Regime Live

Iran confirmed on May 16, 2026 it will soon unveil full details of a transit toll mechanism for the Strait of Hormuz. Iran has formalized a permit-based transit process for vessels passing through the Strait of Hormuz, administered by the newly established Persian Gulf Strait Authority (PGSA), which Tehran created to regulate maritime traffic through the Strait of Hormuz. Reports indicate vessels have paid up to $2 million per transit, settled in Chinese yuan. Israel-linked vessels are banned outright, with U.S.-linked and other "hostile country" vessels facing severe restrictions.

PGSA launched 18 May · India-flagged cluster cleared 18 May · 2 transits on 20 May vs. 95 baseline · Administrative phase operational
Production · Asia

Lotte Chemical Yeosu: On Track for 29 May Restart

Production is scheduled to resume on May 29. Lotte Chemical (011170.KS) disclosed that it will halt operations at all production facilities at its Yeosu plant, with production scheduled to resume on May 29. Lotte Chemical swung to an operating profit in the first quarter, driven by higher selling prices for basic materials amid the fallout from the Middle East war. Lotte Chemical reported consolidated first-quarter revenue of 4.99 trillion won and operating profit of 73.5 billion won.

Scheduled restart 29 May (7 days out) · Ethylene capacity 1.23 Mt/yr · Naphtha FM drivers persist
Distribution · Shipping

Bunker Fuel: Scarcity Premium Holding

Before the war, bunker fuel in Singapore cost about $500 per metric ton. That went up to more than $800 as of early May. Bunker fuel helps move the 80% of globally traded goods that are transported by sea, and experts say that means a shortage of bunker fuel will translate to higher shipping costs, increase consumer prices and hurt the bottom lines of businesses worldwide. In Singapore, the world's biggest refueling hub for bunker fuel, reserves are dwindling and prices are spiking.

Tier 3 Distribution FM flagged Day 74 · $800/tonne confirmed · 60% premium sustained · Asia hub constrained
Geopolitical · Negotiations

Trump: "No Hurry" on Iran Peace Deal

President Trump said he was in "no hurry" to finalize a peace deal with Iran, 12 weeks into the war, while speaking with reporters on Wednesday (20 May 2026). A one-page, 14-point memorandum of understanding (MOU) is being negotiated between Trump's envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner and several Iranian officials, both directly and through mediators. On 8 April 2026, the United States and Iran agreed to a two-week ceasefire in the 2026 Iran war, mediated by Pakistan. Status: ceasefire extended; permanent resolution timeline unknown.

Ceasefire on extension · Negotiations stalled · War option remains live
Market · Commodity

Brent Crude: Elevated on Strait Risk Premium

Brent crude rose +2.43% in 24 hours to $116.73 as the transit collapse sustains supply-risk premiums. Brent crude has responded accordingly, adding +2.43% in the past 24 hours to close at $116.73, with WTI at $112.25. Saudi Aramco CEO Amin Nasser told investors on the company's first-quarter earnings call that the oil market may not normalize until 2027 if the strait's disruption persists past mid-June.

$116.73 Brent · WTI $112.25 · Risk premium embedded · Normalization forecast: post-mid-June IF Strait reopens
Outlook · Scenario Wedge

Three Paths Forward: Deal, Stalemate, or Escalation

Path A (35% probability): Negotiated opening. PGSA toll regime becomes permanent; Strait opens to BRICS + selective Western traffic by Q3 2026. Path B (40% probability): Stalemate persists. Ceasefire holds but Strait remains administratively closed; U.S. naval blockade of Iran continues; dark-fleet economics absorb toll premiums indefinitely. Path C (25% probability): Escalation. Trump order triggers new kinetic phase; IRGC retaliates; Strait closure becomes kinetic again; oil $150+, Wave 4 cascade risk. Current drift: Path B most likely. Restart-type FM count will move to L5 trigger only if Path C occurs or KPC/SABIC extend filings past 25 May.
