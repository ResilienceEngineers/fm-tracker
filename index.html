Strait of Hormuz — Kinetic

17 tanker hits in November; SEVERE sustained

According to a November 26 advisory issued by the Joint Maritime Information Center (JMIC), 17 tankers have been struck in the Strait of Hormuz since the beginning of November. An outbound tanker (Kun Peng) was struck by an unknown projectile off Oman during a transit on 26 November, with one crew member severely injured during the attack. JMIC threat level remains SEVERE; Iran's unilateral mine claims remain unverified by independent sources as of 28 Nov. Kinetic pressure sustained but no escalation to regime-change threshold (would require ≥3 independent operator FMs or multi-day closure).

UKMTO/JMIC official; Threat = SEVERE · 50+ attacks since 1 Oct
QatarEnergy LNG — Force Majeure Extended

FM extended to 31 Dec 2026; regime-change duration confirmed

QatarEnergy declared force majeure on contracts with buyers in China, South Korea, Italy and Belgium, with annual lost revenue estimated at $20 billion. Qatar recorded 41 LNG shipments by November 27, but expected November exports of 3.3 million tonnes remain nearly half of pre-conflict levels. Extended FM to end-December marks a regime-change signal; restart-type FM count now 6 (unchanged from prior brief). Multi-year rebuild of Ras Laffan Trains 4/6 (3–5 years) confirmed; long-term contracts rescission risk (Orlen cancellation 11 Nov) now active baseline.

QatarEnergy/National News 27 Nov · LNG·Restart · Hormuz-linked
Container Port Labor — ILWU Local 26 Settlement

Tentative agreement 21 Nov averts strike; Wave 2 cascade risk removed

The Pacific Maritime Association and International Longshore and Warehouse Union Local 26 have reached a tentative contract agreement covering about 250 watchmen at the ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach, averting an immediate strike threat at the busiest U.S. trade gateway. The agreement remains subject to ratification by Local 26 members; the existing contract will be extended while that process proceeds. Strike would have disrupted 85% of Long Beach and 33% of LA container traffic. Wave 2 (allocation) cascade risk de-escalates with this resolution.

PMA/ILWU official 21 Nov · Container shipping · Distribution (non-Hormuz)
DRC Cobalt Export Ban — Global Critical Minerals FM

Full suspension effective 30 Nov; non-Hormuz cascade trigger

The DRC announced a full suspension of cobalt exports effective November 30, with the ban to be reassessed after four months. Starting November 30, 2026, the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) is suspending all cobalt supply from the global market until further notice. After the DRC announced that it would suspend all cobalt exports from November 30, market expectations of tighter raw material supply next year quickly intensified. This is a non-Hormuz global FM with critical-minerals cascade implications for battery, EV, and semiconductor supply chains. DRC produces ~70% of global cobalt.

Bloomberg/SMM/ARECOMS 23–24 Nov · Cobalt·Regulatory · Non-Hormuz
Southern Natural Gas Pipeline — US Gulf FM

500 Mdth/d capacity cut effective 22 Nov; non-Hormuz event

Southern Natural Gas declared force majeure following a pipeline explosion in Sumter County, Alabama, with transportation capacity on the affected segment reduced by 500 Mdth/d, bringing available capacity down to 1,949 Mdth/d, effective from 22 November 2026. Higher natural gas costs could raise operating expenses for gas-intensive chemical producers, particularly ammonia, methanol, hydrogen, and nitrogen-based fertilizer manufacturers. This is a standalone US Gulf event unrelated to Hormuz crisis; produces regional chemical producer cost pressure.

ChemAnalyst/SNG official 23 Nov · Natural gas · Production (non-Hormuz)
East-West Pipeline — Saudi Aramco Capacity Plateau

4 mbpd sustained; no new damage 26–28 Nov

Saudi Aramco's East-West Pipeline (Strait of Hormuz bypass) maintains ~4 mbpd capacity post-9 November strike on Madinah region. No reported new kinetic events on the pipeline 26–28 November. Pipeline remains critical crude export corridor; Aramco ops maintained. Full recovery to 7 mbpd target remains dependent on sustained air-defense cover and no new Iranian strikes. Baseline bypass function provides offset to Hormuz shipping disruption but insufficient to restore full Gulf export capacity.
