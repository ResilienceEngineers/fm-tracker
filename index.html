Operator FM

QatarEnergy LNG Restart-Type Extension

Qatar extended LNG force majeure into late October, with state-owned QatarEnergy notifying buyers in Pakistan and Bangladesh that cargo cancellations will continue through October . Asian spot LNG touched $23.39/MMBtu (28 Aug, four-year high). Extension signals long-tail production-side restart risk, not new kinetic escalation.

Wave 1 (Production-side FM), Type 5 (Restart), Tier 1 — QatarEnergy press / buyer notification. Hormuz-linked: True.
Shipping Blockade

Hormuz Transit Collapse Sustained

The Strait of Hormuz is effectively closed to commercial shipping as of 30 August 2026, with only 3 ships transiting on August 23 versus ~85 per day normal . IRGC blockade remains active. US counter-blockade of Iranian ports sustained. Iran-Oman accord framework agreed in principle (early Aug) but unsigned through 31 Aug; diplomatic signal remains Medium, not Hard.

Wave 2 (Allocation/Shipping), Type 2 (Shipping), Tier 1 — Straits.live AIS tracking + Lloyd's List. Hormuz-linked: True.
Red Sea Kinetic

Houthi Amzan Attack Claim (24 Aug) — Third in Window

The Houthis claimed an attack on the Saudi oil tanker Amzan off Yanbu in the Red Sea on 24 August, the latest targeting of Saudi assets in support of Iran, heightening fears of escalation across the region . Crew safe, vessel operationally recovered. Attack confirms ongoing harassment; does not signal systemic escalation above baseline rate (one every 2–3 days since May).

Wave 2 (Allocation), Type 2 (Shipping), Tier 1 — UKMTO incident + Houthi claim. Hormuz-linked: True.
Inland Shipping (Non-Hormuz)

Rhine Record Low — Kaub 20 cm

Water levels at Kaub, Germany, a shipping chokepoint, dipped to 20 cm on 5 August—5 cm below the previous record in 2018 . German companies report growing disruption, with chemical producers, utilities and steelmakers warning of higher costs, transport bottlenecks and curbed production; traders said cargo shipments are no longer bookable after weeks of drought . Independent European supply shock, not causally linked to Hormuz crisis.

Wave 3 (Physical absence / distribution), Type 4 (Distribution), Tier 1 — BfG (Bundesanstalt für Gewässerkunde) gauge + trader reports. Hormuz-linked: False.
Diplomatic Stall

Iran-Oman Accord Unsigned Through 31 Aug

Iran-Oman negotiations on Strait management show agreement on route coordinates, but Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said the agreement was "very close" while cautioning that full reopening depends on the US staying true to commitments from the June MoU . No Hard signal of collapse or breakthrough; Medium confirmatory signal only. Trump administration continues to hold firm on blockade enforcement as of 30 Aug.

Wave 2 (Allocation/Diplomatic), Type N/A, Tier 2 — Iran Ministry of Foreign Affairs statements (8 Aug, last public). Hormuz-linked: True.
Panama Canal (Non-Hormuz)

El Niño-Driven Transit Cuts Confirmed

Panama Canal shipping may face renewed pressure over the next 12 months if El Niño weighs on water levels; in previous El Niño episodes, shippers routed more freight through the US west coast and moved goods onward by rail . Transit cuts (34 vessels 3–14 Sept, 32 from 15 Sept onward) confirmed independent of Hormuz crisis. Structural, not Hormuz-linked FM event.

Wave 3 (Physical absence), Type 4 (Distribution), Tier 2 — S&P Global Platts / Panama Canal Authority notices. Hormuz-linked: False.
