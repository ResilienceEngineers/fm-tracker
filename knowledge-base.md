## 7. Internal sources / not-to-do list

- Don't conflate "FM declared" with "production stopped". Some FMs are forward-looking (KPC FM2, Trinseo eff. date in future).
- Don't use Indonesian / Indian / Vietnamese FMs as evidence for sovereign-level escalation. They're Wave 3 cascade markers, not Wave 1.
- Don't publish helium claims without Airgas / QatarEnergy / USGS triangulation. Helium is the highest-leverage commodity but also the most rumour-prone.
- Don't treat container-shipping suspensions as restart-able in days. Once Clause 17 / Clause 10 surcharge clauses activate, recovery is contract-cycle dependent (45–90 days minimum).

## 8. Glossary

- **Wave 1 / 2 / 3** — Felsberger Three Waves model. Production / allocation / physical-absence.
- **FM type** — six categories: Production, Shipping, Downstream, Distribution, Restart, Cascade. Tracker assigns one per declaration.
- **Restart-type FM** — second FM declared at restart to ration physical output even after partial reopening. KPC FM2 is the canonical example.
- **Forward-coverage FM** — FM declared on contracts that haven't been called yet because the operator knows they cannot perform when called. SABIC Tadawul filing.
- **Sovereign-level FM** — FM declared at state-owned producer level on long-term sovereign contracts. QatarEnergy 5-year is the only confirmed instance.
- **Hormuz-linked** — root cause traceable to Strait of Hormuz disruption, including kinetic damage at Gulf-side facilities and shipping-route blockade. Excludes unrelated technical FMs (e.g., Vynova rail closure, Trinseo Tessenderlo technical malfunction) — these are tracked separately as comparator base-rate FMs.
