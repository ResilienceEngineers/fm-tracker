### 02 · Kinetic / facility damage

**Status: Worse (↑)**


SABIC filed a formal Tadawul disclosure on April 8 stating it "cannot provide, at the present time, an estimate for the return to production, as this is contingent on domestic and international factors"
. First Saudi company to place legally-binding war-damage uncertainty on public record under CMA listing rules. Forces continuous disclosure on subsequent financial filings.


EGA said preliminary assessment indicates it will take at least 12 months to restore production at Al Taweelah
 smelter. No update in last 72h; timeline unchanged since Day 41 assessment.


The IRGC pre-announced Jubail as a "direct and legitimate target" on March 18 and specifically named Sadara, the Aramco-Dow joint venture
, signaling that targeting intensity on petrochemical facilities remains framed as reciprocal and intentional, not collateral.

**Why this matters:** SABIC's "cannot estimate" language forces unquantified liability on PIF-Aramco-SABIC-Sadara chain at Q2 year-end. EGA's 12-month timeline and Sadara's joint-venture uncertainty create multi-quarter supply floor. IRGC framing matters for insurance claims and future de-escalation assumptions.

**Implication:** Plan around kinetic-damage floor: Jubail/Sadara offline multi-quarter, EGA offline through April 2027 minimum, Ras Laffan 3–5yr rebuild. Strait reopening alone does not restore these facilities. Insurance and reconstruction financing will be contested on targeting intent vs collateral damage grounds.

**Implication:** Do not assume substitution lanes will absorb indefinite Hormuz closure. Korea's export restriction has ~30–60 days buffer; Libyan and Russian substitutes have existing customers. Track methanol spot prices and bunkering infrastructure FIDs as leading indicators of long-term diversification strategy.
