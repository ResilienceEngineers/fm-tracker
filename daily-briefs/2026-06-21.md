## Summary

The Trump-Pezeshkian memorandum of understanding, signed 18 June, formalizes intent to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Iran has ordered all vessels to submit transit requests 48 hours in advance via the Persian Gulf Strait Authority (PGSA). No charges will be applied for 60 days; costs for security and insurance are covered by the Iranian government. Officials indicated a fee system could be introduced after the 60-day period ends. Three Saudi VLCCs successfully transited the Strait on 19 June and reactivated AIS transponders in the Gulf of Oman. QatarEnergy is ready to resume LNG production at Ras Laffan and could restore output from unaffected facilities to current capacity within a month. No new force majeure declarations from Tier 1 operators reported 19–22 June; restart commitments remain conditional on sustained transit safety.
