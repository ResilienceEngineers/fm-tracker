Cascade through downstream chains

  • Refined products shortage — diesel: Jazan 400,000 bpd offline extended to 30 August (34+ days to date). Russian refinery outages (Ukraine drone attacks) + Middle East disruptions combine. IEA forecast: global diesel inventory falls below 50-day coverage (red line) by 25 August if Jazan/Yanbu output unrecovered. Rationing protocols activated in Japan, Singapore, Rotterdam. Truck freight surcharges up 35–40% vs baseline.
Why this matters: Rhine crisis is now delivering Wave 3 cascade independently of Hormuz. Chemical-major production is operationally constrained (not demand-constrained as in prior Waves). This mirrors the Ras Laffan damage cascade (Wave 1: LNG offline → Wave 2: container shipping suspended → Wave 3: Asian crackers feedstock-starved). Rhine is now executing a parallel path: transport offline → cracker margins crushed → production curtailment.
Implication: Polyethylene and polypropylene spot prices (Europe) will rise 8–15% over next 14 days if Rhine stays below 15 cm through 30 August. Automotive and packaging buyers will activate vendor hedging protocols. BASF's guidance for H2 2026 margin compression is now materialising faster than consensus expected. If Rhine recovery is delayed into October (BfG scenario if drought extends), EU converter curtailment cascades to H1 2027 PET/PTA supply deficits, affecting apparel and beverage packaging chains globally.

This is the transition from Wave 2 (allocation/routing workarounds) to Wave 3 (physical absence — "we cannot make this product at margin").
Implication: Buyer contracts with EU converters will see force majeure notifications in 7–10 days if Rhine stays below 15 cm. Polyethylene / polypropylene buyers will activate secondary suppliers (Middle East, India, China) at 12–20% premiums. Packaging, automotive, apparel chains will pass costs downstream; consumer price impact likely by Q4 2026. If substitution window closes (Rhine + Hormuz + US LNG capacity all saturated), industrial production will enter rationing mode for 8–12 weeks.

Methodology

This brief uses a tiered signal-weighting framework. Only verified physical events from primary sources can move the Wave Intensity assessment. Trend movement requires confirmed change versus the prior 72 hours. The system continuously calibrates against outcomes — when predictions miss, the underlying heuristics are sharpened. The full operating ruleset is kept private to preserve analytical edge.

Sources

Inputs are graded across six tiers before they can move the assessment. Specific outlets are not published.

Tier 1 — Primary, official, verifiable: operator press releases and FM letters; stock-exchange filings; regulator notices; sovereign and central-bank communications; AIS-confirmed vessel movements.
Tier 2 — Specialised commercial intelligence with multi-year operational track record across energy, petrochemicals, maritime, and metals desks.
Tier 3 — Analytical institutions and multilateral commodity desks with rigorous publication standards.
Tier 4 — Commercial analysts and named columnists with auditable forecast records.
Tier 5 — Wire services and reputable regional / sector press.
Tier 6 — Excluded by default: anonymous OSINT, op-eds, and AI-summarised aggregator output. Admitted only with independent Tier 1–3 corroboration.
